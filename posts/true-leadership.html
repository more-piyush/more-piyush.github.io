If you lead a team, the fastest way to lose their trust is taking the credit when things go right and pointing fingers when they go wrong.

Bill Belichick built the Patriots Dynasty around one ideology: Players win you games and Coaches lose the games.

True Leadership isn’t for individuals looking for appreciation. It’s for people who do 4 things:

  1. Give autonomy: Trust isn’t saying, it’s giving people the responsibility to make real decisions, without constantly monitoring them.
  2. Over-prepare: Leave no stones unturned. You prepare for each and every scenario, so your team never panics.
  3. Encourage Disagreement: If everyone agrees with you, you will be biased. Strong decisions require constructive discussions.
  4. Absorb Pressure: When things go bad, don’t ask who messed up. When things succeed, give the spotlight to the team.

Next time when goals fall apart, don’t look for someone to blame. Ask what failed, how can we improve and how to prepare better next time.
