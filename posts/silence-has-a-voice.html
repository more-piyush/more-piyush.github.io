Silence has a voice,
at times, it isn’t a choice
listening to the whispers of a silent suture,
swaying between past and future.

Gazing at the night sky
wasn’t about wading through emotions.
It was about past reflections
and carving future ambitions.

A mirror holding growth,
a mirror practicing composure,
a mirror resting in stillness,
a mirror returning satisfaction
without applause.

A reminder of the stripes earned,
wounds healed,
memories made,
and milestones achieved.

It’s not lonely
it’s peaceful.
It’s not closure
it’s protection.
