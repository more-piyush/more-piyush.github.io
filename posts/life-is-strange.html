Life is strange!
Does the pursuit define me,
or does my presence define me?
Each time I begin to pursue,
I face a blank slate
each time I achieve,
I face a blank slate.
Holding the chalk, I realise,
with quiet surprise,
the squeaks and sighs of trembling lines,
is what made the drawing so fine.
Every stroke had a spark,
though made in the dark
every curve held a dream,
rippling through the silent gleam.
The white dust was never vain
it carried hidden gain,
a quiet proof of how you’ve grown
in every mark, your heart is shown.
So it’s neither pursuit nor presence
that defines me
it’s the stroke, the spark,
the curve, the mark
that defines me.
