"Strategy without tactics is the slowest route to victory. Tactics without strategy is noise before defeat."

We often struggle to get our strategies and tactics accurate collectively.

Strategy defines long term goals and give you the path you need to achieve your goals. It is basically your intent and involves extensive research, planning and high level focused thinking to decide which direction to take.

Tactics involves taking concrete actions and steps to implement, in line with your strategy. It is oriented towards taking smaller steps and a shorter time frame in order to maintain efficiency of tasks. They involve best practices, specific plans, resource, etc.

What makes a good strategy? A strategy should reflect on core values of the team, involves input gathering across the team ensuring that the team aligns with the strategy and their targets. A Good Strategy is focused on the desired end result.

The Strategy will frame the decision making within the team and tasks aimed to achieve the end result.

What makes a good tactic? A Good Tactic has clear steps that assist your strategy. It has a specific timeline within which the tasks are completed and its impacts are evaluated. Tactics can be changed based on the success of the strategy.

It is much easier to adjust your tactics to correct the course of your actions than it is to overhaul your strategy, so it is important to ensure that the strategic planning is thorough.

Strategy without tactics won't support you to achieve your goals or be executed. They give you tangible route to execute your goals thereby impacting efficiency in terms of time, resources and money.
